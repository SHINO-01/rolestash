Support

Email support@rolestash.com. A real person reads every message, usually within one business day (Sydney time).

For billing questions, include the email address you used at checkout. To find a receipt or order, use paddle.net.

Plans and prices are on the pricing page, and quick answers are in the FAQ.

Common questions

A job came out wrong or incomplete

Open the Rolestash panel on the posting, then ⋯ → Extraction details. Then email us the page address, and we'll improve support for that site.

The icon does nothing on a page

Some browser pages (like the Chrome Web Store and settings pages) can't be read by any extension. On embedded job boards, open the posting in its own tab and try again.

Moving to a new computer

Free: use Export backup on the board, then Import on the new computer. Pro: sign in on the new computer and your board syncs, across up to 5 devices including your phone through the web board.

Cancelling, refunds and invoices

See the refund policy.

Deleting your data

Delete jobs on the board, or remove the extension to delete everything stored in your browser. Accounts can be deleted from the extension's settings; see our privacy policy.
