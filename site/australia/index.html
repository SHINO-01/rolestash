A job application tracker made in Australia

Rolestash is made in New South Wales, and it's built for the way people look for work here: SEEK and Jora, government job sites, careers pages run on PageUp and JobAdder, and closing dates on many roles.

Made for how jobs are advertised here

  • Closing dates are saved with each job, and the board warns you before they pass.
  • Salaries are kept as the ad shows them, including ranges and whether they're per year, day or hour.
  • Hybrid, on-site or remote is recorded with the location.
  • Autofill puts your details into application forms, including on Workday and SmartRecruiters, without ever submitting for you.

Your data stays close

The free plan keeps your board in your browser, with no account. If you choose sync on Pro, your board is stored in our database in Sydney. Payments go through Paddle, our merchant of record, in your local currency. See plans and prices.

Questions

Does Rolestash work with SEEK?

Yes. SEEK in Australia and New Zealand has its own reader in Rolestash, checked on the live site in October 2026.

Is my data kept in Australia?

On the free plan your board stays in your browser. If you turn on sync on Pro, a copy is stored in our database in Sydney.
