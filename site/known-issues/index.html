Known issues

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Current version: 0.4.0

Problems we know about, who they affect, and what to do until they're fixed. Found something that isn't here? Email support@rolestash.com.

Open

A deletion on one device can undo a later edit on another

Affects: Pro and Advanced, with sync on two devices. Status: fixed in 0.4.1, now in Chrome Web Store review.

If you delete a job on one device while offline and edit the same job on another, the deletion can win even though the edit came later. Until 0.4.1 reaches you (Chrome updates extensions by itself): sync both devices before deleting a job you've recently changed elsewhere.

Undoing a deletion right after it syncs

Affects: Pro and Advanced, with sync on. Status: fixed in 0.4.1, now in Chrome Web Store review.

If you press Undo a few seconds after deleting a job, the job can come back on this device but stay deleted on your others. Until the update: make any small change to the job (add a tag, say) and it syncs back everywhere.

Dragging is slow on very large boards

Affects: boards with hundreds of jobs in a column. Status: fixed in 0.4.1 (now in Chrome Web Store review), which shows 50 cards per column with Show more.

Until the update: archive jobs you've finished with, or use search to narrow the board.

Limitations

Some pages can't be read

Browser pages such as the Chrome Web Store and Chrome's settings can't be read by any extension. On job boards embedded in another site, open the posting in its own tab.

Chrome on desktop only

Rolestash runs in Google Chrome and other Chromium browsers that install from the Chrome Web Store. Edge and Firefox versions are planned. On a phone, Advanced includes a web board.
