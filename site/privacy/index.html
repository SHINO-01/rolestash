Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

In short: the free plan keeps everything in your browser and sends nothing to us. If you create an account (for a trial or Pro), we store only what's needed to run it. We don't sell data, show ads, run analytics or use AI on your information.

Who we are

Rolestash is a browser extension and web service operated by a sole trader trading as Lumetrix Technologies (ABN 41 649 439 228), New South Wales, Australia ("Rolestash", "we", "us"). Contact us at support@rolestash.com. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, for people in the EU and UK, the GDPR.

The extension on the free plan

  • What it reads: the content of a browser tab, only when you open Rolestash on it (its button on the page, or the Rolestash icon), use its menu item or press its shortcut on that tab. Rolestash has access to the job sites it supports, and to every other site only if you turn on "Show the button on all sites", so its button can sit at the edge of the page. On each page it checks only whether a job posting is open (the address and the page's job data), so the button can say "Save job"; nothing beyond that is read, stored or sent until you open the panel. It does not track or record the sites you visit, and you can hide the button on any site.
  • What it stores: the job details you choose to save (title, company, location, salary, dates, description, posting link) and your own notes, tags and board. These stay in your browser's extension storage on your device. It also remembers there whether it has asked you to rate Rolestash on the Chrome Web Store.
  • What it sends: nothing on its own. On the free plan the extension makes no network requests except to the page you are capturing, and to us only if you send a problem report. Your jobs leave your browser only when you export a file yourself.

Application autofill

Your autofill profile (the details you choose to save, such as your name, contact details, links and saved answers) is stored only in your browser on that device. It isn't synced, isn't in backups and is never sent to us. When you click "Fill this application", it is put into that one page so you can review and submit the form yourself. Autofill never answers equal-opportunity or demographic questions and never submits a form.

If you choose "Fill from résumé", Rolestash reads your PDF or Word file in your browser to pre-fill the profile for you to check. The file and its text are never stored or sent anywhere.

Contacts, interview notes and documents (Pro)

Contacts you add to a job (such as a recruiter's name, email and phone), your interview notes, and the names of documents you sent are stored with that job in your browser, like the rest of your board. Rolestash never stores the documents themselves, only the names and any link you add. If you use sync, they travel with your board. The calendar export puts only times and job titles into the file, never notes or contacts.

Capture from a pasted link (Pro)

When you paste a job link into Add job, Rolestash asks Chrome for access to that one site. It then fetches that one page, without your cookies, and reads the job details from it on your device. If the page needs JavaScript, Rolestash briefly opens it in a background tab instead and closes it when done. Access to the site is removed straight afterwards. Nothing about the page is sent to us.

Connected mailbox (Pro)

If you choose to connect Gmail or Outlook, Rolestash gets read-only access to your mailbox (Gmail's gmail.readonly permission, or Microsoft's Mail.Read). All reading happens in the extension on your computer.

  • What it reads: for new messages in your inbox, the sender and subject, to pick out emails about job applications (Gmail's own search narrows these first). Only those emails are downloaded in full, and only to find what they mean for an application: received, assessment, interview, rejection or offer. Plain rules, no AI.
  • What it keeps: the update it found (for example "interview, Thu 10am, Zoom link"), on the job's card. Never the email. On this computer it also remembers which messages it has already looked at, and your access token.
  • What is sent to us: nothing from your mailbox. Your emails, their senders and subjects never reach Rolestash's servers. If you turn on sync, the job's updated column and the update itself sync like any other change you make.
  • Who else: your computer talks directly to Google or Microsoft to read the mailbox. No one else is involved.
  • Stopping: choose Disconnect in Account. Rolestash then deletes the access token from your computer and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove access from your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.

Rolestash's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use mailbox data for advertising, don't sell it, don't let people read it, and don't use it to train AI models.

Automatic status updates (Pro)

Automatic status updates work in one of two ways, and you choose: connect your Gmail or Outlook mailbox (read-only, read on your computer; see the next section), or get a private forwarding address and choose which emails to forward to it. With the forwarding address, Rolestash never connects to your mailbox. This section is about the forwarding address.

  • What we process: each forwarded email, in memory on our email service, to work out what it means for your application (received, interview, assessment, rejection or offer) using plain rules, no AI.
  • What we keep on our server, briefly: the extracted update, never the email itself. That is the outcome and how sure the rules were; the email's subject, sender, date and message IDs (so replies in the same thread follow); the company, job title, job number and posting links it mentions; any interview time, time zone, location, meeting or booking link; and, for Gmail's forwarding check, the confirmation code. Your board fetches it and then we delete it; anything not fetched is deleted after 90 days.
  • Matching happens on your device: the extension or web board decides which of your jobs an update belongs to. The card keeps the outcome, any interview, and the email's subject, sender and date as the reason, so you can see why it moved and undo it. If you use sync, these travel with your board like the rest of it.
  • Limits: each address accepts a limited number of emails per hour and per day, and mail to an unknown or replaced address is discarded unread.
  • Shared learning: when you accept or correct a suggested update, or file an unsorted one, your board adds non-personal knowledge to a shared list that improves updates for everyone. That knowledge is a one-way fingerprint of the email's template (made with names, companies, titles, numbers, dates and links removed) with its meaning, and which company an email domain belongs to. It never includes email text, subjects or which jobs you applied for. Contributions are recorded under a one-way code instead of your account, and are used only after three different accounts agree. Turn off "Help improve automatic updates" when you create your account, or in Account at any time; that also withdraws what you shared, as does deleting your account.
  • Stopping it: delete your mail filter, get a new address in Account (the old one stops working at once), or delete your account. Updates are deleted with your account.

Problem reports

If you use Report a problem in the extension or on the web board, with or without an account, we receive:

  • your message;
  • an email address for our reply, if you give one (when you're signed in, your account's email is filled in for you);
  • the details shown to you before sending: the Rolestash version, your browser, your plan and where you sent it from, and the page's address only if you tick the box for it;
  • if you're signed in, which account sent it, and a coded (keyed hash) form of your IP address, so no one can send more than 5 reports an hour.

A report never includes your jobs, notes or profile. We store it in our database in Sydney and email a copy to our support inbox through Resend. We delete reports after 12 months at most; if you delete your account first, the report stays but is no longer linked to it.

The web board (Pro)

The web board at rolestash.com/board/ keeps a copy of your synced board in that browser's storage, so it works offline. It talks only to our database in Sydney. Signing out of the web board removes that browser from sync and deletes its copy. It uses no cookies and no analytics.

This website

rolestash.com uses no cookies and no analytics. If you pick light or dark mode, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage; it is never sent to us. Its pages load no third-party scripts, with one exception: the checkout page (rolestash.com/pay/). To take your payment, it loads Paddle.js from Paddle, our merchant of record, which opens Paddle's secure checkout window. Paddle's checkout may set its own cookies and use its own services, such as error reporting, web fonts and translation. Paddle's privacy notice covers these. Our host, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.

Product news emails

If you sign up for product news on rolestash.com (before launch, the "Notify me" form), we keep your email address, the plan you said you're interested in (if you signed up before launch), when you signed up and confirmed, and which of our emails we last sent you. We store it in our database in Sydney and send the emails through Resend. We send:

  • one email asking you to confirm your address;
  • one email saying you're on the list, once you've confirmed;
  • one email when Rolestash launched, if you signed up before launch;
  • occasional product news, such as major new features: a few emails a year at most.

We only email you after you confirm, and you can opt out at any time:

  • One click: every email, including the first, has an unsubscribe link. It works straight away, with no sign-in and no questions.
  • From your mail app: Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail show their own "Unsubscribe" button for our emails.
  • By email: reply to any of our emails, or write to support@rolestash.com, and we'll remove you.

Unsubscribing deletes your address from our list immediately. We keep a confirmed address until you unsubscribe. If you never confirm, we delete it within 30 days. We never share, rent or sell the list, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking: our emails have no tracking pixels or tracked links.

How long we keep information

  • Account and synced data: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete it from our systems within 30 days.
  • Automatic status updates: on our server until your board fetches them, and 90 days at most.
  • Paddle keeps transaction records for as long as tax law requires.
  • Problem reports: 12 months at most.
  • Launch and product news emails: until you unsubscribe, or 30 days if you never confirm.
  • Data in your browser stays until you delete it or remove the extension. Deleting your account does not delete the copy on your device.

Your choices and rights

You can export your board at any time from the extension and delete your account from its settings. You can also ask us to access, correct, export or delete your information by emailing support@rolestash.com; we reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the rights to restrict or object to processing. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or your local data protection authority.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Account data is protected by row-level access rules, so each account can reach only its own records. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Rolestash is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes we'll tell account holders, and people on our email list if the change affects them, by email before they take effect.

How plans, billing and refunds work is in our terms of service and refund policy.
