Job sites Rolestash supports

Open a job posting, click the Rolestash button at the edge of the page (or the icon, Alt+J), and the title, company, location, salary and closing date fill themselves in. The sites below each have a reader built for them. Everywhere else, Rolestash reads the job details the page publishes and flags anything uncertain for you to check.

Applying on a company's own careers page? Most of them run on one of the applicant tracking systems listed below, such as Workday, Greenhouse or Lever, so those pages work too.

Questions

Do I need an account on these sites?

No. Rolestash reads the posting you have open in Chrome. It never signs in anywhere for you.

Does Rolestash read job sites in the background?

No. It reads a page only when you open Rolestash on it: its button on a supported job site, or the Rolestash icon.

What happens on a site that isn't listed?

Rolestash reads the job details the page publishes for search engines, which most careers pages do. If there are none, it makes a best guess and marks anything uncertain so you can check it before saving.

Found the same job on two sites?

It stays one card. Rolestash matches duplicates across sites, so a role you saw on a job board and again on the company's careers page isn't counted twice.

Missing a site you use? Email support@rolestash.com with a link to a posting and we'll look at adding it.
