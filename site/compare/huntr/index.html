Rolestash vs Huntr

Facts checked 4 October 2026

Huntr is a job search platform: a tracker with autofill and AI tools for resumes and cover letters. Rolestash focuses on tracking and autofill, with no AI, no account on the free plan, and as little data collected as it can manage.

At a glance

Choose Huntr if

  • you want AI help writing and tailoring resumes and cover letters;
  • you want to track up to 100 jobs for free.

Choose Rolestash if

  • you'd rather your extension didn't collect web history or activity, and your applications weren't sent to an AI service;
  • you want to start without an account, with everything kept in your browser;
  • you want a lower price for sync and more jobs (Pro is US$12 a month);
  • you search on Australian sites like SEEK and want your synced data kept in Australia.

Both save postings in one click and fill application forms. See the sites Rolestash reads and how it keeps your search private.

Questions

Is Rolestash a good Huntr alternative?

If you mainly use Huntr to save jobs and track applications, and you'd like a tracker that collects less about you and uses no AI, yes. If you rely on Huntr's AI resume and cover letter tools, Rolestash doesn't have them.

Does Rolestash read my browsing history?

No. It reads a job posting only when you open Rolestash on it, and it doesn't collect web history or user activity.

Is Rolestash free?

Yes, for up to 30 active jobs, with no account. Pro is US$12 a month, with everything; see the pricing page for prices in your currency.
