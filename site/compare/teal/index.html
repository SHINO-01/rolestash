Rolestash vs Teal

Facts checked 4 October 2026

Teal is a career platform: a job tracker alongside AI tools for resumes, cover letters and job matching. Rolestash does one part of that, tracking applications, and is built to know as little about you as it can: no AI, no account on the free plan, and job emails read on your computer, never on our servers.

At a glance

Rolestash Teal
Free plan Up to 30 active jobs, no account needed The job tracker is free; an account is needed
Paid plans Pro US$12 a month, with a 14-day free trial Teal+, reported at US$29 a month, US$13 a week or US$79 a quarter
AI None. Saving jobs and email updates run on plain rules AI resume, cover letter and job-matching tools; content is sent to AI service providers for processing
Email and Google account Pro connects Gmail or Outlook read-only and reads job emails on your computer, or reads only the emails you forward Optional Google account connection, with the access you approve
Analytics and ads None, on the site or in the extension Google Analytics and marketing cookies
Where your data is kept In your browser; in Sydney, Australia, if you turn on sync In the United States
Resume builder No Yes

Choose Teal if

  • you want AI help writing and tailoring resumes and cover letters, in the same place as your tracker;
  • you need to track more than 30 active jobs without paying.

Choose Rolestash if

  • you'd rather your applications weren't sent to an AI service;
  • you want to start without an account, with everything kept in your browser;
  • you want status updates from job emails without your mail going through anyone's servers (Pro);
  • you search on Australian sites like SEEK and want your synced data kept in Australia.

Both save postings from job sites in one click. See the sites Rolestash reads and how it keeps your search private.

Questions

Is Rolestash a good Teal alternative?

If what you want from Teal is the job tracker and one-click saving, and you'd rather no AI or Google connection was involved, yes. If you want AI help writing resumes and cover letters, Teal does that and Rolestash doesn't.

Can I move my jobs from Teal to Rolestash?

Not automatically. Rolestash doesn't import from Teal; new postings save in one click as you come across them.

Is Rolestash free?

Yes, for up to 30 active jobs, with no account. Pro is US$12 a month, with everything; see the pricing page for prices in your currency.
