A job tracker that keeps your search private

A job search holds a lot about you: salaries, rejections, notes on recruiters, who you're talking to and when. Plenty of job tools read your inbox on their servers or send your applications to an AI service. Rolestash is built the other way round: it knows as little about you as it can.

What Rolestash never does

  • Never reads your email on its servers. If you connect Gmail or Outlook, job emails are read in the extension, on your computer.
  • Never sends your data to an AI service. Capture and email updates run on plain rules.
  • No ads and no analytics, in the extension or on this site.
  • Never sells your data.
  • Never submits an application for you. Autofill fills fields you can check, and skips demographic questions.

How saving a job works without AI

When you open Rolestash on a posting (its button at the edge of the page, or the icon), it reads the posting in that tab: first the job details the page publishes for search engines, then a reader written for that site if it's one of the supported job sites, then careful rules for anything left. Fields it isn't sure about are flagged for you to check. On the free plan, nothing leaves your browser.

How email updates stay private

On Pro, choose how Rolestash hears about replies. Connect Gmail or Outlook (read-only): the extension looks at the sender and subject of new mail, downloads only job emails, and reads them with plain rules on your computer. Nothing from your inbox reaches our servers, and Disconnect removes the access. Or forward job emails to a private address with a filter, and Rolestash never touches your inbox. Either way it moves the card when it's sure, suggests the move when it isn't, keeps the update and never the email, and every change can be undone.

Questions to ask any job tracker

  • If it reads your email, does it do that on its servers or on your computer?
  • Are your applications or emails sent to an AI service?

  • Can you use it without an account?
  • Can you export everything, and is anything locked if you stop paying?
  • Who runs it, and where is your data stored?

Questions

Does Rolestash use AI?

No. Saving a job and reading forwarded status emails is done by plain rules written for job postings and recruiting emails. Nothing you save is sent to an AI service.

Does Rolestash read my email?

Only if you connect your inbox, and then only job emails, on your computer: nothing from your inbox reaches our servers. Or forward job emails to a private address instead, and Rolestash reads only what you forward. Either way it keeps the update it finds, never the email.

Do I need an account?

No. The free plan works without an account and keeps your board in your browser. An account is only needed for Pro's sync, phone access and email updates.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export everything to CSV or JSON at any time, on every plan. If a subscription ends, nothing is deleted or locked.
