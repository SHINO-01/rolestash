# rolestash.com home

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a job seeker who has applied to dozens or hundreds of roles and lost track. Action: Add to Chrome (the only CTA; the product-news form stays as a quiet secondary strip). Proof: verifiable product facts only; slot for real beta quotes later. Constraints: static HTML, strict CSP, no scripts, self-hosted fonts, brand colours fixed, header/footer shared byte for byte.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Order after chaos. The page itself is calm and ordered, the way the board makes a messy search feel. It refuses the category default of a centred hero, eyebrow labels, a bento of feature cards and a pricing wall.

OWN-WORLD: Existing palette kept (warm off-white ground, spruce ink, amber as the one highlight, mint washes; deep spruce-ink band). Bricolage Grotesque, one family, text and display optical sizes (Geist was tried and dropped as overused). Hairline rules, generous whitespace, real product UI as the only imagery, authored mini-UI artefacts (capture form, card with a deadline, a "what we know" ledger).

STORY: one promise (know where every application stands) → three benefits in a zig-zag, each with an artefact → proof as checkable specifics on the ink band → FAQ answering cost, sites, AI, inbox, data → one closing ask.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left-aligned headline over a wide board screenshot that bleeds off the right edge; lede and the Add to Chrome button sit beside the headline; fine print states free plan and no account.

SIGNATURE: the board rises into place on load (opacity, translate, blur, strong ease-out), then a "Saved to Rolestash" toast settles on it. At the owner's request ("make the site more alive"), two scroll-linked explanations follow: the save form types itself in row by row, and a card is dragged from Applied into Interviewing. State transitions only otherwise: FAQ answers open by height, the mobile menu grows from its button. Nothing else moves.

FORM: no concept roll; the established brand world (palette, product imagery) was kept by the owner's brief, so this is a restructure and type change inside it, not a new world.

RISK: little colour drama; the warmth must come from type and the artefacts, not decoration.
